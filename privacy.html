Rugs Zone — internal workspace of Rugs Creation · Last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Rugs Zone is the internal work app of Rugs Creation, Khewat No 168, Vill Alipur Khalsa, Tehsil Gharaunda, Distt. Karnal, Haryana 132114, India (www.rugscreation.com). It is used by our staff to manage tasks, approvals and daily work.

What we use WhatsApp for

We send work messages to our own staff on WhatsApp through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (by Meta): task reminders, follow-ups and questions that need a reply (for example “Is this done?” or “Please share the date”). We do not send marketing messages through this service.

Information we process

  • Staff name, department and work mobile number, as saved in Rugs Zone.
  • The text of the work messages we send, and the replies or button choices staff send back (for example “Done”, a date, or a short answer).
  • Delivery information from WhatsApp (sent, delivered, read, failed).

How we use it

Only to deliver work reminders and to update the related task in Rugs Zone (for example marking it done or saving the date given). We do not sell or share this information with anyone for advertising.

Who can see it

Authorised Rugs Creation staff who use Rugs Zone, and our service providers that host the app (Supabase) and deliver the messages (Meta / WhatsApp), only as needed to provide the service.

How long we keep it

Messages and replies are kept with the task history for as long as the task record is needed for our work, and are removed when the record is deleted.

Your choices

Staff can ask to stop receiving WhatsApp work messages, or ask to see or delete their message data, by contacting the address below.
