## PPC rules (Production Planning dashboard)
- One colour per SKU (15 Nov 2026), worked out from the data - do not trust the sheet's Auto Status when it conflicts:
  1. RED = overdue: ship date passed (first filled of Ship Date (2) → Effective Ship Date → Revised Ship Date → Ship Date) AND Ship date Actual empty. Red always wins.
  2. Otherwise the sheet's Auto Status (Purple, Yellow, Orange, Green, Light / Medium / Dark Blue).
  3. Auto Status blank or #REF! and not late → Green.
  Only one chip per SKU ("Red - overdue"; no second "Late" chip). The Late count, the Overdue Only filter and the colour counts all use this same rule, so Red count = Late count.
- Owner rule (30 Oct 2026, kept): on a production owner's own link, a SKU at Weaving/Tufting/Knitting Finish or any later stage is not late for him - it keeps its sheet colour instead of Red (`sheetStatus`), and is left out of his Late count.

- 15 Nov 2026 (late evening): both faults above are now FIXED AND LIVE, and a third, deeper one was found and fixed on the way. `rugs-mail` is **v38**. ROOT CAUSE of the sync trouble: **Gmail answers HTTP 204 with an empty body when a time window has no mail in it** (Google does that whenever a short `fields=…` reply would be an empty object); the code ran `JSON.parse("")` on it, so "nothing found" was handled as a Gmail failure — that is where the hundreds of "Gmail sent an incomplete reply" errors came from, and in v36 the window was then marked done and its mail never fetched (quiet boxes like neetu@ / vinay@ failed on nearly every run, never reached "live" and stopped loading older history). v38 treats an empty reply / 204 as "nothing in this window". Results after deploying: errors 8-13 per minute → **0**, run time 53-57 s → 25-30 s, all 6 mailboxes **live · history complete** with no "could not list", and a 12-hour re-read of every mailbox found **nothing missing** (so no mail was actually lost today — the "mail not coming" feeling came from the Switch ID session, where only Team Alpha's mail is visible).
